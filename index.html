What the vlogs taught us

Distilled from 21 Barcelona and València vlogs: book Sagrada and Park Güell before flying (August sells out), treat La Rambla as pickpocket championship level, eat the menú del día at lunch and paella only at lunch, markets close at three, and horchata fixes everything else. August sea: 26 ° Barcelona, 27 ° València. Pack GHIC cards, real sunscreen, and one empty bag for market loot.
